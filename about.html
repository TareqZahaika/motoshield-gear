FAQ

How do I choose the right safety level?

Daily riders can start with Level 2 protection. Sport or highway riders should choose higher impact protection where possible.

Can products be customized?

Yes. Size, color, initials, protection level, material, and order notes can be saved during checkout.

Does the site process real payments?

No. It is a university demo that stores the selected payment method and status in Supabase.
